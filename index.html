About

关于我们

HARTHELM，由 Hearth（家）与 Helm（掌舵）合成：为家族理清方向，为企业稳住节奏。

我们面向内地高净值客户，在香港与新加坡两地，提供家族安排的咨询、规划与协调，以及企业出海相关的运营协同。

涉及持牌业务时，我们转介并协同律师、会计师等持牌第三方，不自行提供受规管的投资、信托或税务筹划服务。

一个对接窗口，把选项、流程与边界讲清楚，陪您把规划落到日常执行。

HARTHELM brings together hearth (home) and helm (steering): clarity for the family, steady pace for the enterprise.

We serve mainland Chinese high-net-worth clients in Hong Kong and Singapore with consulting, planning, and coordination for family arrangements, alongside operational support for outbound ventures.

Where licensed work is required, we refer and coordinate with licensed professionals—we do not provide regulated investment, trust, or tax-optimization services ourselves.

One point of contact: options, process, and boundaries made clear—then walked through with you.
