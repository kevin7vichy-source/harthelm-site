No outcome is promised

Any visa, pass, stay, extension, settlement, or right-of-abode application is decided by the authority, at its discretion. Personal data

If you email office@harthelm.com, or contact us through the WhatsApp link on this site, we receive what you choose to send, including your email address, WhatsApp number, and the text of the message. We use it only to reply and to follow up the matter you raised. We do not sell personal data.

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. The host may log a visitor’s IP address and similar technical data for security. When this page loads Google Fonts, your browser sends your IP address and browser information to Google. Those services’ servers may be outside Hong Kong and Singapore. We are in Hong Kong.

If you contact us on WhatsApp, WhatsApp / Meta processes the related data under its published terms and privacy policy, and the data may be transferred outside Hong Kong. GitHub, Google, and WhatsApp / Meta process data under their published terms. We have not entered into a separate data-processing agreement with them.

Personal data is handled under Hong Kong’s Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (Cap. 486). Where it relates to Singapore, it is also handled under Singapore’s Personal Data Protection Act 2012.

You may withdraw consent by emailing office@harthelm.com. We will tell you the consequences and stop the processing that depends on that consent. After withdrawal, we may be unable to continue replying to that enquiry or providing the related service. Withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out.

To ask for access or correction, email office@harthelm.com, and state your name and what you are asking for. The personal data officer, who is also the data protection officer, handles that request. Do not attach passwords, full bank-account numbers, or images of identity documents.

In Hong Kong, a data-access request is made under section 18 of the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance and is generally complied with within 40 days after it is received (section 19(1)). A correction request is made under section 22(1). If a fee is charged, we tell you the amount first. In Singapore, you may ask to be told what personal data of yours we hold and how it was used or disclosed in the past year (section 21 of the Personal Data Protection Act), ask for a correction (section 22), and withdraw consent (section 16).

Enquiry data from people who do not sign is kept for 24 months from the last contact, and then deleted. A client’s data is kept during the service and for 7 years after it ends, including to meet the accounting-record period in section 377 of Hong Kong’s Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622). It is kept longer where the law requires, or while an enquiry, complaint, or dispute is still open, until that matter ends. When the data is no longer needed for the purpose, we take practicable steps to delete it.

Unless you give a separate consent, we do not use the name and contact details you gave for an enquiry to send service information or other direct marketing, and we do not provide that data to anyone else for direct marketing. If we later propose to do so, we will first inform you and obtain consent under Part 6A of the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (sections 35C and 35E). You may email office@harthelm.com at any time and ask us to stop. There is no charge.

If personal data about you is breached, we assess it and take remedial steps promptly. Hong Kong’s Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance sets no statutory deadline for notifying a breach. Where Singapore’s Personal Data Protection Act, sections 26C and 26D, requires notification, we notify the Personal Data Protection Commission within 3 calendar days after the assessment, and we notify affected individuals when the law requires it.
