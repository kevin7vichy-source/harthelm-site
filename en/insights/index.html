Top Talent Pass Scheme

Compiled 10 November 2026. Status: current. Not legal advice.

  • A job in Hong Kong is not required at the time of application.
  • Category A: annual income of HK$2.5 million or above in the year immediately before the application.
  • Category B: a bachelor’s degree from an eligible university, and at least three years of work experience in the five years immediately before the application.
  • Category C: a bachelor’s degree from an eligible university within the past five years, with less than three years of work experience. An annual quota and first-come handling apply.
  • The first period of stay is generally 36 months for Category A and 24 months for Categories B and C.
  • Immigration generally takes about four weeks after the documents and the fee are in. Meeting the criteria does not remove the Director’s absolute discretion to refuse an individual application.
  • An extension of stay requires proof of employment in Hong Kong with a stable income, or of having established or joined a business in Hong Kong.
  • The list of eligible universities is updated yearly. The government notice effective 1 January 2026 is linked below. This site does not turn processing speed into a promise.
  • Immigration: Top Talent Pass Scheme
  • Guide ID(C)1026 (marked 04/2026)
  • Eligible universities (effective 1 January 2026)

Quality Migrant Admission Scheme

Compiled 10 November 2026. Status: current. Not legal advice.

  • The scheme is still run by Immigration.
  • An applicant is generally at least 18, able to support himself or herself, of good character, and usually holds a recognised degree.
  • The General Points Test uses published criteria. The current test is 6 items out of 12, and the official guide controls.
  • The Achievement-based Points Test requires exceptional achievement. A job offer is not required at application.
  • Patterns of stay, and any arrangement for top-tier talent, follow Immigration’s latest page. Month-counts circulating in the market are not an official limit, and this site does not present them as one.
  • Immigration: Quality Migrant Admission Scheme
  • Guide id(c)982

Admission Scheme for Mainland Talents and Professionals

Compiled 10 November 2026. Status: current. Not legal advice.

  • For mainland residents who already have an offer of employment in Hong Kong.
  • The applicant needs skills or experience that Hong Kong requires and that are not readily available locally.
  • A relevant bachelor’s degree is usually required. In special cases, professional ability or experience may be shown instead.
  • The post must be genuine, the work related to the degree or experience, and the package broadly in line with the market.
  • Unlike the Top Talent Pass and Quality Migrant Admission, employment is generally required first. See the ASMTP page on this site.
  • Immigration: Admission Scheme for Mainland Talents and Professionals
  • Guide ID(C)991

Immigration Arrangements for Non-local Graduates (IANG)

Compiled 10 November 2026. Status: current. Not legal advice.

  • For non-local students who have studied full-time in Hong Kong on a locally accredited programme and obtained a bachelor’s degree or higher. Other arrangements, including eligible Greater Bay Area campuses, follow the official page.
  • Fresh graduates who apply within six months of graduation generally do not need an offer first, and are usually granted 24 months on the first permission.
  • A later application generally requires employment already secured, with the post and pay meeting the rules. An extension of stay also requires employment.
  • A student entry and IANG are two arrangements, one after the other. Starting a course is not the same as already holding a Hong Kong status. See the IANG page.
  • Immigration: IANG

New Capital Investment Entrant Scheme

Compiled 10 November 2026. Status: current. Not legal advice.

  • The applicant must first show Invest Hong Kong that, for the six months before the net-asset assessment is filed, net assets of at least HK$30 million (or equivalent) were held throughout.
  • The applicant then invests at least HK$30 million in permissible assets under the scheme rules. That includes a HK$3 million placement into the portfolio managed by the Hong Kong Investment Corporation, and related rules, as the official site states them.
  • Eligibility, entering as a visitor to complete the investment, approval in principle, and later formal approval are separate steps.
  • An extension of stay still has to meet the scheme rules, including maintaining the investment. It is not a status with no later conditions.
  • Mainland Chinese residents are generally outside the scheme. It is generally for foreign nationals, Chinese nationals who have permanent residence abroad, and Macao or Taiwan residents, among others, as the official site states.
  • From 1 March 2026 there is an update on private holding-company arrangements. See the FamilyOfficeHK page.
  • Immigration: New CIES
  • New CIES: investment requirement
  • FamilyOfficeHK: arrangements from 1 March 2026

Entry for study

Compiled 10 November 2026. Status: current. Not legal advice.

  • Full-time study in Hong Kong is applied for under Immigration’s entry arrangement for non-local students.
  • An offer from a school is not itself permission to work or to settle.
  • A dependant application has to meet the policy in force at the time.
  • A school’s own admission rules are on that school’s site. This site does not repeat admission details that were not checked item by item.
  • Immigration: overview of admission schemes
  • EdUHK postgraduate programmes (the school’s own page for its requirements)

Exit endorsement for settlement in Hong Kong or Macao

Compiled 10 November 2026. Status: current. Not legal advice.

  • From 1 July 2026, the notice sets out four categories of conditions.
  • 1. Reunion of husband and wife, and accompanying children: separation from the spouse settled in Hong Kong or Macao for three years.
  • 2. A child joining parents: the applicant is under 18, and both parents are settled in Hong Kong or Macao.
  • 3. A child caring for parents: the applicant is 18 to 59, both parents are settled in Hong Kong or Macao, both are 60 or over, and they have no children there.
  • 4. A parent joining a child: the applicant is over 60 and has no children on the mainland, and the child settled in Hong Kong or Macao is 18 or over.
  • The public security authorities still decide according to the quota and the conditions. The notice does not say that meeting the conditions means approval.
  • National Immigration Administration notice
  • Hong Kong and Macao Affairs Office reprint

Singapore Employment Pass

Compiled 10 November 2026. Status: current. Not legal advice.

  • An Employment Pass is a work pass for foreign professionals. It is not permanent residence or citizenship.
  • Family members who qualify may apply for a dependant’s pass. That is not the same as the family holding Singapore residence status.
  • The applicant must meet the qualifying salary, which rises with age and differs by sector, and pass COMPASS (40 points), or fall within an exemption.
  • As published at the time of this note, for new applications before 1 January 2027: from S$5,600 a month in non-financial services, rising to S$10,700 at age 45 and above; from S$6,200 in financial services, rising to S$11,800. From 1 January 2027 the bar for new applications rises further. MOM’s page controls.
  • Employers generally still have to meet the Fair Consideration Framework and related hiring rules. Salary is not a figure to be chosen in order to collect points.
  • Permanent residence is a separate decision. Holding an Employment Pass for a period of years does not guarantee it.
  • MOM: Employment Pass eligibility
  • MOM: COMPASS salary benchmarks

Singapore corporate service providers and video checks

Compiled 10 November 2026. Status: current. Not legal advice.

  • The Corporate Service Providers Act 2024 took effect on 9 June 2025. A business providing corporate services in Singapore must register with ACRA.
  • A nominee director must be arranged by a registered corporate service provider, and anti-money-laundering duties apply.
  • For remote registration and similar cases, the provider must hold a real-time video call, and keep the record, with a proposed director who is not a nominee, with a member holding at least 50%, or with an authorised representative.
  • The corporate and LLP miscellaneous amendments were announced as effective on 16 June 2025. The two sets of rules did not start on the same day.
  • This page does not quote maximum fines. That check did not match them line by line to the statute.
  • ACRA announcement 865
  • Corporate Service Providers Act 2024
  • ACRA announcement 872 (miscellaneous amendments)

Türkiye investment citizenship: property threshold

Compiled 10 November 2026. Status: current. Not legal advice.

  • The public Turkish official note on citizenship by investment still lists real-estate investment of USD 400,000, and a period during which the property may not be sold.
  • This page states that threshold only. Marketing claims that sit beside it are not repeated, and citizenship is not written as an expected result.

US EB-1A, basic framework

Compiled 10 November 2026. Status: current. Not legal advice.

  • EB-1A is the extraordinary-ability category in US employment-based immigration. The basic framework is still at least three of the ten criteria, plus a final review of the record as a whole.
  • The applicant must show extraordinary ability and that he or she will continue to work in that field in the United States.
  • Processing and premium-processing times change. That check did not match them to the USCIS timetable then current, so this page states none.
  • Filing another application is not an extra assurance of a result.
  • That note did not record a specific USCIS URL, so this item has no link, rather than a URL that was not part of the check. Read the EB-1 page then on uscis.gov.
