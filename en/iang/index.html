Immigration Arrangements for Non-local Graduates (IANG)

IANG is the arrangement for non-local graduates to stay or return for employment. It is not the student visa. Full-time locally accredited study at bachelor level or above, and this arrangement after graduation, are two stages. Starting the course, or even graduating, is not the same as already holding a Hong Kong status that allows settlement.

A fresh application filed within six months of graduation generally does not need an offer first, and the first permission is usually 24 months. After that window, an application generally needs employment already in place, with the post and pay meeting the rules. A later extension of stay also requires employment. Other cases, including eligible Greater Bay Area campuses, follow the Immigration page.

Family members do not receive a status automatically because someone starts a course. A dependant application has to meet the policy then in force. The free self-check does not include IANG. Company and office support during a gap is described in the service scenes on the home page. The outcome is still for Immigration.
